A drought that never happened

The gauge reported 10 days out of 31, and nine of them read zero. A blind trigger adds that up to 6.1 mm, calls it a drought and would pay ₦2,500,000. Pluvia sees 21 missing days and a satellite that measured 131.7 mm, and sends an inspector.

Four outcomes

The model explains.
The code decides.

A model reads the findings and writes the case. Plain code applies the contract, so no wording can clear a failed check or set a payout.

  • Pay

    The rain failed.

    Both records agree the farm went dry. The farmer is paid.

  • Pay anyway

    Rain hit the gauge, not the farm.

    A distant station cannot refuse a farm that went dry. Pluvia pays it.

  • Inspect

    The record has holes.

    Gaps or disagreeing sources send an inspector to walk the field.

  • Do not pay

    The rain came.

    The crop stood. Every refusal still carries the strongest case for paying, for an officer to read.
